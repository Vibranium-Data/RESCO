# RESCO Examples

RESCO is widely applicable across urban monitoring systems in both data-rich and resource-constrained environments.

The following examples show RESCO's applicability across a variety of domains including climate / environment, mobility, energy, water and housing / real estate. RESCO can be used for one particular system, e.g. a utility or bus transit system, or can be implemented across multiple systems e.g. at the city administrator level to observe, monitor and implement different indicators and interventions across multiple urban systems.
