# RESCO Examples

RESCO is widely applicable across urban monitoring systems in both data-rich and resource-constrained environments.

The following examples show RESCO's applicability across a variety of domains including:

- Climate / environment
- Mobility
- Energy
- Water
- Housing / Real estate
- Logistics

RESCO can be used for one particular system, e.g. a utility or bus transit system, or can be implemented across multiple systems e.g. at the city administrator level to observe, monitor and implement different indicators and interventions across various urban systems.
